# One purchase. Your servers, easier to reach.

Unlock all premium setup features permanently through Google Play. Your existing premium purchase already covers AWG 3.1 and Tailscale.

## AWG 3.1 guided setup

Set up an obfuscated, full-tunnel AmneziaWG 3.1 VPN on your own VPS. cookbox.ai prepares the installer choices and pairing flow, so you spend less time assembling commands and copying connection details.

Useful when you want your phone traffic to use your VPS and need an obfuscated VPN transport. The app connects through the tunnel by itself; the AmneziaWG app is only needed for other devices.

## Tailscale guided setup

Reach your Linux PC, WSL machine or VPS through your private tailnet, without forwarding SSH ports on your home router. Guided installation and pairing help cookbox.ai use the right tailnet address.

Useful for working on a machine at home or moving between networks. You still control your Tailscale account; the app joins your tailnet by itself with an auth key, so the Tailscale app can stay off.
